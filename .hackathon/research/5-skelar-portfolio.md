# SKELAR portfolio and how its support works: almost all SKELAR businesses sell consumer subscriptions (mostly web-funnel subscriptions billed in the US and other English-speaking markets). Their support is in-house, runs 24/7 in shifts on Zendesk or Freshdesk, and is mostly about billing: refunds, cancellations, unrecognised or double charges, chargebacks. The best demo products are Liven, Brighterly and RiseGuide, which can share one billing and chargeback desk.

### (b) How support is organised today (from job postings)
- **Team shape: in-house, 24/7, shift-based, English-speaking agents.** The agents are Ukrainian; the users are "в основному з американцями" (mostly Americans). Every posting asks for night shifts (23:00–08:00 or 19:00–03:00). — high (https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/SKELAR/681ea5c9-f8ce-4ca3-8bd7-d9c189758587, https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/SKELAR/f60519ab-8281-4a87-af7e-d49e56a91b07, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/330742/)
- **Tools: Zendesk is the default, Freshdesk second; Intercom is named once.**
  - Billing roles ask for "ticket-based системами (Zendesk або аналоги)" (ticket-based systems, Zendesk or similar).
  - The Dressly posting wants "helpdesk платформою (Zendesk або Freshdesk)".
  - Two agent postings say "дзвінки, чати та email (Freshdesk)" (calls, chats and email).
  - GDx asks for "Freshdesk/Zendesk/Intercom та їх налаштування" (and configuring them).
  - Live help centres confirm it: Liven, RiseGuide and Relatio use Zendesk Guide; Leaply, Dressly and HabitONE embed Zendesk; MaxBeauty uses Freshdesk.
  — high (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/375385/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/375717/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/330574/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/342039/, https://support.theliven.com/hc/en-us, https://support.riseguide.com/hc/en-001, https://help.getrelatio.com/hc/en-us)
- **KPIs are named explicitly in the RiseGuide posting:** "Розуміння ключових метрик підтримки: First Response Time, Resolution Time, CSAT, Refund Rate" (understanding of key support metrics) and "Знання Zendesk — від роботи з тікетами до налаштування макросів, тригерів, тегів, аналітики та бази знань" (Zendesk knowledge, from handling tickets to setting up macros, triggers, tags, analytics and the knowledge base).
  - Agent roles add "Утримання refund rate на належному рівні" (keeping the refund rate at the right level).
  - Leads own "дотримання SLA" (meeting SLAs) and "KPI та бонусної системи" (the KPI and bonus system).
  — high (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/320166/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/330574/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/336743/)
- **Typical tasks are refunds and retention.** Quoted: "Робота з утриманням користувачів від рефандів та здійснення рефандів через платіжні інструменти за потреби" (persuading users not to take a refund, and issuing refunds through the payment tools when needed). Another posting gives this example of a hard request: «Я хочу повернення коштів!» ("I want my money back!"). — high (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/329910/, https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/362401/)
- **A dedicated Billing/Payments ops desk is hiring now (Oct 2026).** Duties:
  - "Робота з платіжними інцидентами — failed payments, refunds, disputes/chargebacks" (handling payment incidents)
  - "Розслідування складних платіжних кейсів (unauthorized payments, chargeback threats, підозрілі транзакції, пошук root cause)" (investigating complex payment cases: suspicious transactions, root cause)
  - "Робота з fraud-кейсами та аналіз fraud patterns" (fraud cases and fraud-pattern analysis)

  It has three open variants. Payments itself is a SKELAR Platform team that runs acquiring "для бізнесів будь-якої складності" (for businesses of any complexity). — high (https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/SKELAR/23c0d03a-85dd-484e-830e-2794c5ccaafe, https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/SKELAR/dcce0d8b-2429-4112-ad59-641e4fdbfaa5, https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/SKELAR/7caae883-2872-455f-9975-9b1c08b70054)
- **AI is already on their roadmap, as a human+AI setup:**

  - GDx lead: "Запуск AI в процеси сапорту з метою розвантаження агентів в пікові години" (bringing AI into support to offload agents at peak hours) and "Автоматизація QC процесу" (automating the QC process).

## Evidence against / open doubts
- **Few SKELAR support roles are posted openly right now.** On DOU, most support vacancies are inactive and the live ones are billing/payments, Dressly, Liven and VIP. The bigger support teams may be fully staffed, or hiring may happen elsewhere. (https://jobs.dou.ua/vacancies/feeds/?search=SKELAR)
- **Several support postings hide the business name** ("одну з наших портфельних компаній", one of our portfolio companies). We cannot be sure which product the Freshdesk-plus-refund-retention team or the billing desk belongs to. (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/330574/)
- **Products may already have AI here.** Liven is already training "нашого AI-агента" (our AI agent), and Howly's consumer product is itself an AI assistant. A plain "AI chatbot answers FAQs" demo would add nothing new for them, so the demo has to act and audit. (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/336743/, https://howly.com)
- **Refund rules are discretionary** ("considered individually"), so any policy engine we build is our own reconstruction from public help pages, not their internal rules. (https://support.theliven.com/hc/en-us/articles/32096809042578-How-to-Request-a-Refund)
- **Trustpilot does not show heavy pain:**
  - Liven: 4.3, 35,575 reviews, 13% one-star, 99% of negative reviews answered within about 24h.
  - Brighterly: 4.5, 974 reviews.

  The problem to sell is cost and speed at scale and compliance, not "support is broken". (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/theliven.com, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brighterly.com)
- **Retention offers are risky.** Pushing them inside cancellation flows ("you may see options like getting a free month") is exactly what the FTC targets in the Genesis complaint. Any AI-driven "save" must stay compliant. (https://support.theliven.com/hc/en-us/articles/20437588177554-How-to-cancel-my-Liven-Subscription, https://www.bakerlaw.com/insights/ftcs-genesis-tech-complaint-signals-more-than-another-rosca-case/)

## What this means for the idea
- **Build for a cross-portfolio "Support + Billing Ops" desk, not one app's chatbot.**
  - Inputs: one Zendesk-style ticket queue, a mock payments/subscription API and a policy pack per product.
  - Coverage: the intents that dominate every SKELAR knowledge base (unrecognised charge, double charge, add-on charge, cancel, refund, renewal at full price, charge after cancel, chargeback threat).
  - Fit: this matches the Platform payments team and the billing desk they are hiring for now.
- **Ranking for a one-day demo:**
  1. **Liven.** Largest operation (6 teams, 24/7, Zendesk, phone plus email, already training an AI agent), a public 45-article knowledge base we can load, and messy billing (main plan, separate Livie and Coaching add-ons on 31-day cycles, workbooks, web vs store purchases). Automatable work:
     - classify the intent
     - look up the subscription
     - explain a double charge (authorisation vs add-on vs workbook)
     - cancel the right subscription on the right channel and verify it
     - recommend a refund under the money-back policy, with human approval
     - draft the reply in Liven's tone
  2. **Brighterly.** The best "AI takes real actions" scenario, because the rules are explicit:
     - reschedule a lesson only if it is 6h+ away
     - credit a tutor no-show back to the balance
     - change tutor with a stated reason
     - pause for at most 3 months (4 per year)
     - check refund eligibility (teacher dissatisfaction, 3 months or 24 lessons without progress, family emergency; unused lessons only)
     - turn off auto-renew instead of a mid-period cancel

     Trustpilot shows exactly these complaints (tutor no-show, then "full refund & cancel the 3-month membership").
  3. **RiseGuide plus the shared Billing/Chargeback desk.** RiseGuide's posting names the KPIs to put on our dashboard (FRT, Resolution Time, CSAT, Refund Rate) and has a 97-article knowledge base. The billing desk adds:
     - dispute and chargeback triage (refund before the dispute when a refund is cheaper)
     - unauthorized-payment and fraud-pattern flags
     - root-cause notes

  PawChamp is covered by the other researcher. It is a chat/email dog-training consulting team (Zendesk or Freshdesk) and works better as a "content advice" scenario than an actions one (https://jobs.dou.ua/companies/skelar/vacancies/330742/).
- **The QC and analytics half of the challenge maps onto real roles.** SKELAR has QC specialists, and GDx wants "Автоматизація QC процесу" (QC automation). So our evaluation module should:
  - auto-score each ticket against a rubric (correct policy, empathy, actually performed the action, compliance)
  - roll the scores up to agent and shift KPIs
  - flag knowledge-base gaps from recurring intents (Liven CX Specialist role)
- **Make compliance a feature.** The demo should show the AI actually executing the cancellation and confirming it, and refusing to claim "cancelled" without an API confirmation. That ties straight to the FTC Genesis allegations, which SKELAR judges will recognise. Keep retention offers optional and disclosed.
